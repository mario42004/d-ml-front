# Prompts de imagen para la presentacion d-ml

Guarda las imagenes generadas en `docs/presentations/images/` con los nombres indicados. Formato recomendado: PNG, horizontal 16:9 o cuadrado amplio, sin texto dentro de la imagen.

## 1. `01_sound_to_evidence.png`

**Uso:** portada.

**Prompt:**

> A sophisticated editorial image showing a luminous sound wave transforming into structured clinical and computational evidence, with subtle data nodes, waveform lines and human-centered technology, elegant Barcelona research atmosphere, premium scientific presentation style, warm but serious lighting, no text, no logos, no stock-photo look, 16:9.

## 2. `02_crossroads_bsc.png`

**Prompt:**

> A thoughtful senior research engineer standing at a symbolic crossroads with three paths representing research, technology transfer and entrepreneurship, subtle Barcelona city and research campus atmosphere in the background, cinematic but realistic, strategic and hopeful mood, refined color palette, no text, no logos, 16:9.

## Variantes de estilo si Nano Banana responde demasiado generico

Usa este sufijo al final del prompt:

> Style: premium research strategy deck, realistic but slightly editorial, restrained colors, elegant composition, no cartoon, no exaggerated sci-fi, no text, no watermark.

## Paleta visual recomendada

- Fondo claro o blanco roto.
- Verde sobrio para salud y crecimiento.
- Azul profundo para investigacion y computacion.
- Toques dorados para decision estrategica.
- Evitar imagenes oscuras, stock medico generico o pantallas con texto falso.
